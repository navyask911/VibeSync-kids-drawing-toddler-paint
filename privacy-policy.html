Privacy Notice

This notice describes the current app implementation. It is not legal advice or a certification of compliance. Parents and guardians should review the Play Store disclosures, Data safety form, third-party provider terms and this notice before use.

Children and parent controls

Settings, purchase actions, subscription management, external support links and export/backup actions are placed behind an arithmetic parent gate. This is a user-interface control, not identity verification, age assurance or verifiable parental consent.

Artwork and local storage

Drawing strokes, templates, stamps, fill layers and drawing preferences are automatically saved to app-private device storage. This repository does not implement developer-operated accounts, a cloud sync backend or automatic artwork uploads. Parents may explicitly export/share files through the system file picker, print service or share sheet. Any recipient selected by a parent processes the file under its own policies. Local data may be lost when app data is cleared, the app is uninstalled or the device is reset.

Creative Agent and voice

The Creative Agent uses on-device prompt/story dictionaries and non-identifying drawing tags. Text-to-speech is provided by the installed device speech engine. The app does not request microphone permission, record a child's voice or upload prompts, voice recordings, artwork or stroke coordinates to a hosted LLM in this implementation. Device speech providers may have their own service behavior.

Advertising, billing and other providers

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads, Google Play Billing, connectivity checks, in-app updates, file selection, PDF/printing, image encoding and text-to-speech libraries. Ad requests set child-directed treatment, a G-rated content ceiling and a non-personalized request extra. These code settings do not certify SDK/mediation eligibility or prove compliance with Google Play Families, COPPA, GDPR or other laws. Verify the active SDK certification status, disclosures, regional consent requirements and Play Console declarations before release.

Google Play processes purchases and may receive transaction, account, device and diagnostic information under its own policies. For Android's one-device subscription policy, the app reads Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID locally through a native platform channel and computes a SHA-256 digest salted with the app package name (com.navya.kids_drawing_studio). The raw Android ID is not sent to the publisher's server; the digest is passed to Google Play Billing as the obfuscated account ID for adfree_yearly. Google Play stores that value with the purchase and returns it in Android purchase records so the app can compare it with the current device.

A successful device-matched subscription confirmation and the 64-character binding hash are cached in SharedPreferences for up to 7 days for offline startup. A cache is ignored when its stored hash does not match the current device. Android documents ANDROID_ID as scoped to app signing key, Android user/profile and device on Android 8.0+ and stable across uninstall/reinstall while those scopes remain unchanged. A factory reset, signing-key change or different Android user/profile may produce a different identifier.

A legacy/test purchase with no Play obfuscated account ID may be bound locally on first activation. Without a Play-side identifier or server-side record, that fallback cannot prove which device first claimed it after app data is removed. This repository has no server-side purchase-token verification or central device-claim service. Client-side binding is not tamper-resistant proof and cannot guarantee enforcement against a modified client, identity changes, expiry, refunds, chargebacks or revocation; stronger enforcement requires a trusted backend.

Connectivity and update APIs may contact their providers' network endpoints. This repository does not operate a backend receiving these checks.

Permissions

The Android app uses Internet and network-state permissions. It does not request microphone or precise-location permission. It uses app-private storage and parent-initiated file picker/share/print flows rather than broad shared-storage access.
