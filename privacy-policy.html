Privacy Policy for VibeSync Kids Drawing Toddler Paint

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

VibeSync Kids Drawing Toddler Paint is a child-oriented drawing app. Parents and guardians should review this policy and the app's Google Play privacy disclosures before use.

2. Information and Artwork

The app does not implement user accounts, a developer-operated backend, drawing uploads or cloud synchronization. Drawing documents are automatically saved in the app's private local storage on the device. The stored document can include stroke points, each stroke's color/style/width, sticker placements, the drawing-pad background, and locally created paint-bucket fill layers. This auto-save feature does not upload artwork to the developer.

Drawings remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Exporting drawings to shared storage, a photo gallery, or a cloud service is not currently implemented. The app also stores the selected language locally.

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads. Google Mobile Ads and related ad services may process technical, diagnostics and advertising-interaction data subject to the provider's policies, the app's configuration and applicable law. The absence of a developer-operated backend should not be understood as a guarantee that no data is processed.

3. Advertising and Child-Directed Settings

The app configures child-directed ad treatment, a G-rated content ceiling and a non-personalized-ad request extra. These technical settings are safeguards, not a certification or guarantee of compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K or Google Play Families Policy. Ad serving, network configuration, parental-gate behavior and the Play Console declarations must be reviewed before release.

The current Flutter UI includes a parental gate before attempting to unlock bonus content through a rewarded ad. The interstitial helper is present in the code but is not currently called from the drawing screen.

Google's policies and service information: Google advertising technologies and Google Play Families Policy.

4. Permissions

The Android app requests Internet and network-state permissions for online ad requests, update checks, and optional external links. Drawing recovery uses app-private local storage, so the app does not need photo, media or shared-file permissions for this feature. Shared-storage export is not implemented.
