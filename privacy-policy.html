Privacy Policy for VibeSync Kids Drawing Toddler Paint

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

VibeSync Kids Drawing Toddler Paint is a child-oriented drawing app. Parents and guardians should review this policy and the app's Google Play privacy disclosures before use.

2. Information and Artwork

The app does not implement user accounts, a developer-operated backend or cloud synchronization. The current drawing (including strokes, stickers, pad color, selected template and raster fill layers) is automatically saved to app-private storage on the device and restored after an ordinary app restart. This autosave is local and is not uploaded by this app. It may be removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; previous-session undo history is not retained.

A parent-gated export option renders the current canvas to a temporary PNG on the device and opens the operating system share sheet; the image is passed only to the app selected by the parent/user. That recipient app may process the image under its own practices. This app does not upload exported drawings to a developer-operated server.

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads. Google Mobile Ads and related ad services may process technical, diagnostics and advertising-interaction data subject to the provider's policies, the app's configuration and applicable law. The absence of a developer-operated backend should not be understood as a guarantee that no data is processed.

3. Network Status and Optional Support

The app checks internet reachability so it can display an offline notice and retry available update checks. These checks may contact external network endpoints provided by the connectivity-checking dependency. Drawing strokes are not uploaded by this feature. The optional support link is only presented after an arithmetic parent gate and opens an HTTPS page configured by the developer.

4. Advertising and Child-Directed Settings

The app configures child-directed ad treatment, a G-rated content ceiling and a non-personalized-ad request extra. These technical settings are safeguards, not a certification or guarantee of compliance with COPPA, GDPR-K or Google Play Families Policy. Ad serving, network configuration, parental-gate behavior and the Play Console declarations must be reviewed before release.

Bonus coloring templates are behind an arithmetic parental gate. A rewarded ad is used only when a production rewarded ad unit has been configured for the release; otherwise a parent can unlock the template without an ad. Google's test rewarded/interstitial identifiers are only used by debug builds. The interstitial helper is present but is not currently called from the drawing screen.

Google's policies and service information: Google advertising technologies and Google Play Families Policy.

5. Permissions

The Android app requests Internet and network-state permissions for ad functionality and connectivity checks. The app uses app-private storage for autosave and temporary export files and opens the native share sheet for user-initiated sharing, so it does not request broad photo, media or file-storage permissions.
